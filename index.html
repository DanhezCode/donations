A LITTLE CONTEXT

Why I chose to build independently.

I got into programming through curiosity, experimentation, and a lot of learning on my own. When I started looking at software development as a career, the usual path into remote work turned out to be more complicated than I expected.

That pushed me toward independent work. Open source became one of the ways I could keep building software and sharing what I was learning along the way.

You can read more about that story in The practical side of remote work ↗ and Why I started building independently ↗ .
